Another Blog Post

October 10, 2026 by Tariq Dacres

When I decide to play Minecraft, I mainly play survival because I am not as versed as the rest in building those crazy landscapes and other luxurious infrastructures.

I mainly play survival, as it creates awareness as well as survival skills to progress you not only in the game but also in an indirect way towards life. It all starts with: how much time do I have and what do I need? Then once you have gathered that information, it is time to get out there and hunt wood, food, dirt, stone and so forth to make your hut, have something to eat and have artillery to fend off any creepy crawlers.
